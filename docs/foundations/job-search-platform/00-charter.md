# Charter: Job Search Platform Upgrade

## Problem Statement

Current job discovery and filtering experience is functional but not yet a complete search product. Users need richer controls, better relevance, and broader source coverage.

## Vision

Deliver a high-signal, low-friction job search system that supports complex search intent, source diversification, and actionable next steps for resume and outreach workflows.

## Outcomes

- Improved search relevance and transparency.
- Better user control over filtering and targeting.
- Expandable adapter model for public and partner sources.
- Faster iteration with safer governance and delivery practices.

## Non-Goals (Initial)

- Automated job applications without policy/legal review.
- Source integrations that violate terms of service.
- Building paid partner connectors before validating demand.

## Design Principles

- Search-first UX over run-first UX.
- Explainability over opaque ranking.
- Progressive complexity: simple by default, advanced by choice.
- Compliance-aware integration strategy.
- Reversible architecture decisions.

## Success Metrics

- Search session to relevant shortlist rate.
- Median time to first relevant result.
- Filter usage and saved-search reuse.
- Source reliability and freshness.
- Resume or outreach generation conversion from search results.
