# Governance and Operating Model

## Purpose

Define how product and engineering decisions are made, tracked, and reviewed for the job search platform upgrade.

## Roles

- Product Owner: prioritization, user outcomes, acceptance criteria
- Tech Lead: architecture integrity and tradeoff decisions
- Engineering: implementation, testing, observability
- Reviewer: compliance and policy checks for sources

## Decision Governance

- Material product or architecture decisions are logged in RAID.
- Scope changes require update to delivery plan.
- High-impact source additions require policy review sign-off.

## Meeting Cadence

- Weekly Delivery Review
- Weekly RAID Review
- Bi-weekly Architecture Review
- Monthly Strategy and Pivot Review

## Change Control

A change requires a decision record when it affects:

- Source acquisition method
- Data schema or API contracts
- Ranking behavior
- Security and compliance posture

## Reporting Artifacts

- RAID log updates
- Delivery plan status
- Open issues and blockers
- Top risks and mitigations

## Escalation Path

1. Raise blocker in RAID issues table.
2. Assign owner and target resolution date.
3. Review in weekly delivery meeting.
